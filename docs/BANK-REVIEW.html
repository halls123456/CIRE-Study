The original 3,000 entries contained 124 underlying questions. We removed 2,876 copies that repeated the same full question and answer choices with generic introductions or different option ordering. Another 41 originals were excluded because their wording was misleading, outdated or insufficiently qualified. Those originals and their review reasons remain in the archive.

What the new bank covers

Every one of the 99 numbered outcomes has at least one newly authored question. The new set includes calculations, comparisons, scenarios and ethics. This is not a claim to cover every syllabus bullet or a substitute for the official curriculum. Questions and explanations are independent practice content, not official exam questions. Retained originals have not had a full specialist validation.

Mock exams retain 110 questions, 120 minutes and the syllabus's indicative element weights. The 60% target is an app practice setting, not a verified official passing score.
